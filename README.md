# Bad Seed Botanicals: Interactive Investor Pitch

A 15-slide interactive presentation for HEMP 203 (Course Project Part 1). It runs in any browser with no installs or build tools. Just four files.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | All 15 slides, the speaker notes, and the toolbar |
| `style.css` | The comic-book look: paper, ink frame, rainbow lettering, characters |
| `app.js` | Navigation, the talk timer, and every interactive feature |
| `README.md` | This page |

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (about 5 minutes)

1. Sign in at [github.com](https://github.com) (or make a free account).
2. Click **+** (top right), then **New repository**.
3. Name it something like `bad-seed-pitch`. Set it to **Public**, then click **Create repository**.
4. On the new repo page, click **uploading an existing file**.
5. Drag in `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, and `README.md`. Click **Commit changes**.
6. Go to **Settings**, then **Pages** (left sidebar).
7. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**. Set **Branch** to `main` and the folder to `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
8. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the Pages screen. Your link shows up at the top. It looks like this:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/bad-seed-pitch/`

That link is what goes in the discussion post.

To change something later, open the file on GitHub, click the pencil icon, edit, and commit. The site updates in a minute or two.

## Presenting and recording

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| → or Space | Next slide |
| ← | Previous slide |
| N | Show/hide speaker notes (your script, with cues for what to click) |
| O | Slide overview, to jump anywhere |
| F | Full screen |
| H | Hide the toolbar for a clean screen recording |
| T | Reset the talk timer (it turns orange at 9:00 and red at 10:00) |
| ? | Shortcut list |

On phones and tablets, swipe left or right. Turn the phone sideways for the best view.

To link straight to one slide, add `#slide-7` (or any number) to the end of the address.

**Recording tip:** press **F** for full screen and **H** to hide the toolbar. Keep the notes open on a second screen or a phone using the same link. Then record your screen (Zoom, Loom, or your school's recorder) while you click through.

## What's interactive

1. **Cover:** tap each product character so it says hi.
2. **Same plant, different job:** a THC line tester. Drag Δ9-THC and THCA and watch the hemp/marijuana stamps change under the old rule and the new total-THC rule.
3. **25,000+ products:** the counter runs up. Tap the stalk, seed, or flower to light up the markets each part feeds.
4. **Varieties:** a matching game. Pair each product with the plant bred for it.
5. **Product line:** flip cards with how each product is made.
6. **Grow the season:** you're the farmer. Make two growing calls per product, hit Grow, and watch which crops thrive or flop (and why).
7. **Making Spritz Happens:** a six-station extraction lab. Harvest the flowers, take CO₂ supercritical, winterize out the wax, hold-to-heat decarb, distill THC to non-detect, then spritz-test the bottles.
8. **Making Grain Damage:** wipe on three coats and watch a microscope view change each time (soak, oxidize, cure). Run the water test before and after, plus a pop quiz.
9. **Making Dry Humor:** a stalk-to-towel mill. Pick harvest day, crank the decorticator, sort bast vs. hurd, dial in the hemp-cotton blend, and weave the towel.
10. **Regulations:** a deck of rule cards. Tap the top card to toss it to the back, or jump to any level. Live countdown to 11 Dec 2026.
11. **Nothing gets wasted:** an animated flow diagram from one plant to every product, with the leftovers becoming animal bedding, livestock feed, and compost.
12. **Market:** an animated USDA figure.
13. **Why invest:** reveal the fixes, then crash markets. A one-legged stool (one-lane brand) topples, and Bad Seed's four-legged stool wobbles and stays up. Illustration, not a forecast.
14–15. **References:** APA 7, with clickable links. Hit **Happy investing!** for the finale.
